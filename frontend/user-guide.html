Using HD IT Pulse

Pulse is the front door to HD IT's internal apps. Sign in once with Microsoft 365 and open everything you've been given access to.

Signing in

Select Sign in with Microsoft and use your HD IT Microsoft 365 account. You'll be asked for MFA if your account needs it. Pulse remembers you in this browser until you sign out from your account menu (your initials, top right).

If Microsoft says your account isn't set up for Pulse, you haven't been added to any Pulse group yet. Ask a Pulse administrator.

Opening an app

Home and the Apps menu list the apps you can use. Select one and it opens inside Pulse, already signed in. Everything works as it does on its own: Help, Refresh, Settings for administrators.

The menu collapses to icons while an app is open, to give it room. Use the button at the bottom of the menu to expand or collapse it.

Your name in Autotask

Changes you make in an app are recorded in Autotask under your name. Pulse finds you by matching your Microsoft 365 email to your Autotask resource. If they don't match, the app asks you to choose your name once. You can change it later from the app's account menu.

Your access

Your access (in the account menu and under Platform) lists your roles, what each gives you, and which apps you can open. Roles come from groups in Microsoft Entra ID, for example Pulse - Whitespace Editors. After an administrator changes your groups, sign out and back in.

Dashboards

The greyed-out dashboards on Home show what's planned for each division. They switch on one at a time as they're built.

For administrators

To give someone access, add them to the right group in the Entra admin centre (Groups → the group → Members). The full list of groups and what each grants is on Your access for Pulse platform administrators, and in docs/IDENTITY-AND-ACCESS.md in the Pulse repository.

Tip. Platform administrators are administrators in every app. Keep that group small.

If something's wrong

  • An app says you don't have access: you're missing that app's group. Ask an administrator, then sign out and back in.
  • An app keeps saying it's signing you in: reload Pulse. If it continues, sign out and back in.
  • Your name in an app is wrong: use Change my Autotask name in the app's account menu, or ask an administrator.
