HD IT Pulse · what's new · v0.6.3

What's new in Pulse

Every release, newest first. Each one is tested in the test environment before it reaches production.

9 November 2026

0.6.3

  • Link tasks: Project Managers tick tasks in the order they should happen and link them in one go. Pulse shows the new dependencies (and any tasks that need to move) before saving them to Autotask.
  • Remove a dependency from a task's panel.
9 November 2026

0.6.2

  • Resource planner: everyone's project hours against what they have available, week by week, with over- and under-allocation highlighted. Expand a person to see which tasks fill each week.
  • Project lead time (also for Sales): how much work is queued, how fast the team gets through it, and the week a new project could realistically start. Add people in the what-if to see how much sooner.
9 November 2026

0.6.1

  • Log time on your project tasks from the Gantt chart, and mark them complete. Both go straight to Autotask.
  • Project Managers can drag bars to move a task or change its dates. Dependent tasks move with it; Pulse shows every change before saving to Autotask, and you can undo it straight after.
  • Save baseline to compare the plan against later slippage.
9 November 2026

0.6.0 Projects

  • Projects, under Managed Services: every open Autotask project on one Gantt chart, grouped by client, with late and at-risk projects highlighted and the work remaining across all of them.
  • Select a project for its own Gantt chart: phases, tasks, milestones, dependencies, the critical path and the baseline, with who's assigned and the hours worked.
  • Logging time, completing tasks and dragging bars to reschedule arrive in 0.6.1; the resource planner and lead time in 0.6.2.
9 November 2026

0.5.4

  • Apps under their division: Pipeline, Marketing ROI and Whitespace Matrix now sit under Sales & Marketing in the menu.
  • Seats serviced and New seats signed now fill in automatically on trial, from Autotask contracts and won opportunities.
  • FY plan: MRR, Seats and New seats month-end figures fill in on trial from Autotask. Leadership or Finance switch them on once they match.
  • One Autotask connection: Pulse uses a single Autotask API user and limits what each part of Pulse can do with it.
3 November 2026

0.5.3 Automatic values

  • Automatic values on trial: Pulse now works out SLA first response, tickets closed per tech per day, first-contact resolution and billable utilisation from Autotask every hour. They show under the typed-in values so you can compare; the typed-in value still counts.
  • Select a cell to see how the number was worked out. Select a measurable's name to compare recent weeks side by side.
  • Leadership can switch each measurable to automatic once it matches, and can ask Pulse to work the values out again straight away.
3 November 2026

0.5.2 People

  • People and departments (Leadership and Platform Admins): see who's in each Autotask default department. Service Desk is marked, because those are the techs the Scorecard counts.
  • Change default departments for one or several people at once. Pulse updates Autotask straight away and keeps a record with your reason.
  • Directory: everyone in Pulse matched to their Autotask account by email. Platform Admins can fix a match or mark that someone has no Autotask account.
3 November 2026

0.5.1

  • Connections: Platform Admins can now enter, replace and remove the credentials Pulse uses (Autotask, SimpleSAT, Xero, Datto RMM, IT Glue) straight from Pulse. Autotask details are tested before they're saved. Pulse never shows a credential again.
3 November 2026

0.5.0 EOS core

  • Scorecard: the company's 15 weekly measurables, 13 weeks at a time. Type values in while a week is open; after it locks, override with a reason. Leadership can change any measurable; everyone else their own division's.
  • Rocks with milestones, the Issues List with top-three priorities, To-dos, and the V/TO.
  • Accountability Chart: all 23 seats from the August 2026 chart, with roles, key results and who sits in them.
  • Divisions and seats: each division has a page with its seats, apps and numbers, plus its own Scorecard, Rocks, Issues and To-dos. Each seat has a page with its roles, people, measurables, Rocks and apps.
  • Home now shows your seat, your To-dos, your Rocks and the measurables you own.
  • FY27 progress: the leadership team sees Revenue, MRR, Seats and New seats against the 1-year plan, with a bar for each quarter. Finance enters month-end figures under FY plan.
3 November 2026
